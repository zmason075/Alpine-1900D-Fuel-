▶What this means

    Top-down view, nose up. The wing diagram (from your ATS poster) is the left wing, overlaid on each wing. Click any pump icon in the diagram to fail or restore it. Click an ENG box to cycle OFF, START, RUN. Click a firewall valve (FW) to close or open it. Click the aux float valve to stick it. Click an electrical bus badge in the fuselage to remove power. Fuel panel and circuit breakers (bottom, laid out like the cockpit poster): click a switch position to move it, click a breaker to pull or reset it. What each breaker does when pulled is my assumption. Engine feed path, as on the poster: collector, firewall valve, engine-driven low pressure pump, fuel/oil heat exchanger, high pressure pump, FCU, injectors. Rates that are not in your notes are my assumptions and are easy to change: aux pump 1000 lb/hr into the collector, jet pumps 1500 lb/hr each, gravity flapper valves 900 lb/hr. The total and the quantity gauge count the main and aux tanks only (4484 lb full); collector fuel is not added. Not modeled: vent and thermal expansion overflow, water drains, ferry fuel provisions, electrical detail beyond the three buses that power the pumps. Always verify against your approved flight manual.
